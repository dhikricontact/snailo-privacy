Privacy policy — Snailo

Last updated: 26 October 2026 — Publisher: SPARTL APPS — contact: spartlapps@gmail.com

In short

Snailo collects no personal data. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no crash reporting. It works fully offline and has no server.

What is stored on your device

Only on your device, to make the game work: your settings (language, text size, sound, music, vibration, rain, reduced animations), your progress (levels, stars, shells, garden, golden fireflies), your daily-trace results and streak, and your hint balance. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What is sent anywhere

Nothing. The "Share" button of the daily trace only hands a short text (for example "Snailo — Trace #41 — in one go ⭐⭐⭐") to the share sheet of your device when you tap it; you choose where it goes, and Snailo never sees it again. The daily level comes from the date, not from a server.

Two buttons in Settings open your web browser when you tap them: this privacy policy and the Cozylo website (cozylo.games). Nothing is sent from the app when they open.

Permissions

None beyond the ordinary: vibration feedback and sound playback. No camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage access, and no internet access is needed to play.

Children

Snailo has no ads, no purchases with real money and no data collection. Golden fireflies and hints are earned by playing and cannot be bought.

Your rights

Since nothing about you is collected, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. Questions: spartlapps@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the new text is published at the same address.
